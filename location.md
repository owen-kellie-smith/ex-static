# Bicton College

{% include gallery.html size="xxl" href="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/owen-kellie-smith/ex-static@v0.0.6/assets/images/bicton_front.jpg" %}

EX Music Summer School takes place at Bicton College in East Devon. 
Set in 200 hectares of open green parkland and rolling countryside, the  majestic house overlooks a beautiful lake. 

En suite accommodation is available within the grounds and includes breakfast and an evening meal. 
Camping, motor-home and caravan facilities are also available and food (breakfast and evening meal) can also be provided if desired. 

If you live locally, or would prefer to source your own accommodation, we also welcome daily, non residential participants.
