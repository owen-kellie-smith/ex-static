# Courses

## Symphonic Wind Band
Open to players from **Grade 5 standard to advanced**.

Participants rehearse a wide repertoire and work with experienced conductors and coaching staff.

## Intermediate Wind Band
Open to players from **Grade 3–5 standard**.

Focus on ensemble playing and development of musicianship in a supportive environment.

## Conducting Course
A practical course for conductors at different levels.

Includes:

- Podium time with the bands
- Observation of rehearsals
- Conducting guidance and feedback
